## 📘 What is Master-Darknet?

**Master-Darknet** is a comprehensive, "Zero-to-Hero" knowledge base designed to demystify the **Dark Web**, **Deep Web**, and **Decentralized Networks**. Unlike other "Awesome" lists that just provide links, this repository offers structured documentation, practical labs, and philosophical analysis.

It bridges the gap between **Cybersecurity theory** and **Real-world application**, covering:
* **Anonymity Networks:** Tor, I2P, Freenet, Lokinet, Yggdrasil, Nym, Mixnets.
* **Infrastructure:** Hidden Services, ISP surveillance, Metadata protection, and P2P/F2F architecture.
* **Operational Security (OPSEC):** Identity protection, OSINT evasion, and cryptographic foundations.

Whether you are a researcher, student, or privacy enthusiast, this project provides the **concepts, philosophy, and practical skills** needed to navigate hidden networks safely.

## 📚 Curriculum & Practical Resources

This project is structured like an academic curriculum (**Parts → Modules → Sections**), moving from fundamental concepts to advanced network operations.

### ✅ Currently Covered Topics
* **Foundations of Hidden Networks:** Deep Web vs. Dark Web taxonomies.
* **Network Architectures:** Comparative analysis of Tor vs. I2P vs. Freenet.
* **Alternative Networks:** Intro to Yggdrasil, Lokinet, and Nym Mixnets.
* **Privacy Philosophy:** Sociotechnical motivations, law, and ethics of anonymity.
* **Infrastructure & Metadata:** Understanding how ISPs track traffic and how F2F networks bypass censorship.

### 🚧 In Active Development
* **Practical Darknet Operations:** Setting up Hidden Services (.onion, .i2p).
* **Hardening Environments:** Configuring TAILS-OS, Whonix, and Qubes for maximum security.
* **Cryptography:** PGP, OTR, and end-to-end encryption standards.
* **Darknet Forensics:** Methods used to deanonymize users (Academic perspective).

> *This is a living textbook. Content is updated weekly to reflect the changing landscape of digital privacy.*

## ⚡ Why This Repo? (The "Awesome" Factor)

Most Dark Web resources are either:
1.  Sensationalist "creepy" stories.
2.  Illegal marketplaces.
3.  Dry, outdated academic papers.

**Master-Darknet** fits the "Awesome" criteria by being:
* **Ethical:** Focused on technology and sociology, not crime.
* **Holistic:** Combines **Philosophy** (Why do we need privacy?) with **Practice** (How do we achieve it?).
* **Modern:** Covers next-gen mixnets like Nym and blockchain-based DNS (Handshake/ENS), not just Tor.
