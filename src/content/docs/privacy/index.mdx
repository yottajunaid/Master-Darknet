# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** Feb 5, 2026

Welcome to **Master-Darknet**. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website. Master-Darknet is a global educational documentation website operated from India. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

This website collects personal information only when you submit it through the contact form. This may include:

- Name
- Email address
- Message content

This information is used solely to respond to inquiries and communications. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by hosting infrastructure and analytics services, including:

- IP address
- Browser type and device information
- Pages visited and timestamps
- Referring URLs

This information is used for website operation, security, and performance analysis.

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

- Respond to contact inquiries
- Maintain website functionality and security
- Analyze traffic and improve content quality
- Display advertising through Google AdSense

We do not create user accounts or maintain user profiles.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, analytics, and advertising.

### 3.1 Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be used to ensure website security and proper technical operation. These cookies are necessary for basic functionality.

### 3.2 Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as IP address and browsing behavior through cookies. This data is aggregated and used for statistical analysis.

Users can learn how Google uses data collected from sites that use its services by visiting Google’s privacy documentation.

### 3.3 Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to display ads to users based on their visits to this and other websites.

This enables the display of advertisements relevant to users’ interests.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users can also control cookies through their browser settings.

A basic cookie consent banner is displayed to inform users about cookie usage.
